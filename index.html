A MODERN ENTERTAINMENT HUB

Everything you enjoy, together.

TolieFlick is an independent Windows desktop application being developed to make it easier to find and enjoy your entertainment. It brings locally stored media and game libraries together with optional connections to online platforms.

It's a work in progress. Features, integrations and availability may change as development continues.
